# Panels and layouts
keywords: layout, layouts, panel, panels, window, dock, float, lighting desk, workspace, tab, reset layout

Every panel opens from the Window menu. Drag a panel's tab to dock it somewhere else or out of the window to float it; close it from its tab.

The Layout menu loads built-in layouts (Default, Lighting Desk, Viewport Only), saves your own arrangement under a name, and deletes saved ones. Lighting Desk puts the playbacks, cues, effects and audio under the viewport, and groups, palettes, the assistant and the Inspector on the right.
