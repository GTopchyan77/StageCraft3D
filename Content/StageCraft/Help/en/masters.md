# Grand Master and Blackout
keywords: grand master, gm, master, blackout, black out, dark, all lights off, masters
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Playback
control: Blackout

The Grand Master (GM) fader in the Playback panel scales every light's intensity last, whatever plays or is held in the programmer. A stage always opens with it at full.

BLACKOUT takes every light to zero at once; click it again to bring them back. The Blackout command has a keyboard shortcut too (see Preferences › Keys).

If every light is dark, check BLACKOUT and the Grand Master first.
