# Getting fixtures from GDTF Share
keywords: gdtf share, gdtf-share.com, download fixture, fixture library, online fixtures, personality, profile, manufacturer file, user file, download manager
panel: StageCraft.Panel.GdtfShare

GDTF Share (gdtf-share.com) is the free online library of GDTF fixture files from manufacturers and users. StageCraft can search it and download files for you, straight into the fixture import report.

- Open it: File > Get Fixtures from GDTF Share... You need your own free gdtf-share.com account; sign in with its user name and password.
- Your password goes only to GDTF Share, over a secure connection. StageCraft never saves it, so you sign in again after a restart. GDTF Share also ends a session after two hours without use.
- Search by manufacturer, fixture name, revision or uploader. Every word you type must match. MANUFACTURER FILES shows only files the manufacturer uploaded; USER FILES only files other users made. Each row says which it is.
- GET downloads a file. Nothing is added yet: the import report opens, shows the modes and channels StageCraft drives, and you choose ADD or CANCEL. A type you already have says IN YOUR LIBRARY; getting it again offers to replace it.
- Downloads are listed under DOWNLOADS with their progress. CANCEL stops one; CLEAR FINISHED tidies the list. Up to four run at once.
- The list is loaded when you sign in and kept for two hours, as GDTF Share asks; REFRESH loads it now.
- Stages that use downloaded or imported GDTF fixtures can be published to the marketplace: choose I MAY SHARE THEM under GDTF FIXTURE FILES, and only if the files' licence allows it. Buyers then get the same fixtures added to their Library.
- Powered by GDTF.
