# Music: sources, tempo and effects that follow it
keywords: audio, music, song, sound, bpm, tempo, beat, file, mp3, input, microphone, system audio, youtube, sync, modulate, trigger, bass, sound to light
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Audio
control: SourceFile

The Audio panel chooses the music the lights follow: FILE... (a song on this PC), INPUT (a line input or microphone), SYSTEM (whatever this PC plays, YouTube in a browser for example) or TEST (a built-in beat). It shows the band meters, the tempo (BPM) and a lamp on every beat.

In the Effects panel, an effect's AUDIO row makes it follow the music:
- SYNC runs the wave at the tempo, on the beat;
- MODULATE lets a band's level (bass, mid, high) scale it;
- TRIGGER steps the pattern on each beat or hit.

The AUDIO quick effects start these ready-made. Without music they hold, or stay fully lit. Input and system audio are analysed in memory only, never recorded or sent.
