# The programmer, Clear, Highlight and Fan
keywords: programmer, select, selection, fixtures, clear, highlight, fan, encoder, inspector, values, live
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Groups
control: ClearProgrammer

Select fixtures in the viewport, the Outliner or a group, then change them in the Inspector or the fader bank: those changes are held in the programmer. The programmer is above the cues and playbacks until you clear it.

- STORE (Cues panel) or REC (a playback) records what the programmer holds.
- CLEAR clears the programmer: fixtures go back to the playing cue and their home look. Undoable.
- HIGHLIGHT shows the selected fixtures at full, in open white, to find them on stage. It is never stored in cues.
- FAN spreads a change over several selected fixtures in selection order: off (all the same), linear, V or its reverse.
