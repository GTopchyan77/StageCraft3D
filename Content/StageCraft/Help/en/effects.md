# Effects: waves, chases and movement
keywords: effect, effects, chase, wave, waveform, sine, square, circle, figure 8, rate, speed, spread, phase, size, wings, blocks, tap, stop all, fx
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Effects
control: Wave

An effect repeats a wave over the selected fixtures. Click a quick effect to start one on the selection, then change it in the Effects panel.
- WAVE: the shape (sine, square, ramps, random, circle, figure 8). Circle and figure 8 move pan and tilt together.
- RATE: cycles per second; TAP sets it from your beat. SIZE: how far pan and tilt swing.
- SPREAD: how far apart the fixtures are in the cycle (360 makes a chase). WINGS mirror it; BLOCKS move fixtures together.

Effects live in the programmer; store a cue to keep them, and they track like any value. STOP stops one effect, STOP ALL every effect (one undo step).
