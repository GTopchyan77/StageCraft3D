# The assistant
keywords: assistant, ai, chat, ask, voice, mic, armenian, online, model, claude, offline, help
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Assistant

Ask the assistant about the stage ("how heavy is the stage?", "list cues"), how to do something ("how do I store a cue?", "where is blackout?") or tell it what to do ("add 6 moving heads", "store cue 1 called Intro", "make it rain"). It understands English and Armenian, typed or spoken (MIC).

Questions about how to do something are answered offline from this help and the app's own controls; SHOW ME opens the panel and points at the control. Requests run through the same rules and undo history as the buttons. An online model can be turned on in the project settings; without it everything works offline.
