# Graphics quality
keywords: graphics, quality, scalability, low, medium, high, epic, resolution, frame rate, fps, frame cap, vsync, tearing, slow, laggy, laptop, fan, performance

Preferences › Graphics sets how StageCraft draws on this PC. Changes apply at once and are remembered.

- Quality (Low, Medium, High, Epic): shadows, lighting, reflections and effects. Lower asks less of the graphics card. On a fast card a large rig is limited by the processor, so a lower quality may not raise the frame rate; on a slower card it usually does.
- Resolution (Auto, 50%, 67%, 75%, 100%): the size the stage is drawn at before it is scaled to the viewport. Lower is faster and softer. Auto lets the engine choose for your display.
- Frame cap (Off, 30, 60, 120): the most frames per second StageCraft draws. A cap keeps a laptop cooler and quieter.
- VSync (Off, On): waits for the display's refresh, so fast moves do not tear, at the cost of a little input delay.

If no Quality button is lit, the saved levels were changed outside StageCraft; picking one replaces them.
