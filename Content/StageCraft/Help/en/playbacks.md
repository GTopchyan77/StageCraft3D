# Playbacks, faders, HTP and LTP
keywords: playback, playbacks, fader, faders, go, flash, rec, rel, release, clr, htp, ltp, priority, wing, desk
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Playback
control: Fader

The Playback panel has ten playbacks, like a console's playback wing. Each has its own cue list and tracks on its own.
- Raise the fader from 0 to start the playback at its first cue; the fader scales its intensity.
- GO steps it to its next cue. FLASH, held, brings it to full until you let go.
- REC records what the programmer holds as its next cue, then clears the programmer. REL releases it (fader to 0, back before its first cue). CLR deletes its cues.

When several playbacks drive one fixture, intensity is HTP (highest takes precedence) and every other attribute is LTP (the latest change wins). The programmer is above all playbacks; the Grand Master scales everything last.
