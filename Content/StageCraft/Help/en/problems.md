# The Problems panel
keywords: problems, diagnostics, errors, warnings, what is wrong, check the show, issues, patch conflict, dmx not working, troubleshoot
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Problems
control: ProblemList

The Problems panel (Window › Problems, a tab in the Lighting Desk layout) lists what may keep the show from looking or working as intended, worst first: overlapping DMX addresses, fixtures without an address while DMX goes in or out, DMX IN or DMX OUT that is off, failed or silent, BLACKOUT on or the Grand Master low, cues and playbacks that name fixtures no longer on stage or palettes that were deleted, effects that follow the music with no music, and what the programmer holds.

It updates by itself as the show changes; REFRESH checks again at once (whether the music is heard is not pushed). Click a problem to go to it: its fixtures are selected and the control that fixes it is shown.

The assistant answers "any problems?" from the same list, and "why is fixture 12 dark?" for one fixture.
