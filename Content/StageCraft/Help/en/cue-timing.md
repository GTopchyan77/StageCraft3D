# Cue timing: In, Out, Delay, Follow and Wait
keywords: fade, fade time, in time, out time, delay, follow, wait, trigger, curve, s-curve, linear, feature time, timing
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Cues
control: RecordFade

Each cue has its own timing in the Cues panel row.
- IN: seconds the cue's values take to arrive.
- OUT: seconds for intensity going down; the same as IN unless you change it.
- DELAY: seconds after the cue starts before anything in it moves.
- Trigger: GO (your Go), FOLLOW (when the cue before it finishes) or WAIT (seconds after the cue before it starts). Click the trigger to change it.

The detail button opens feature times (a fade of its own for position, colour or beam), the wait time and the curve: LINEAR moves evenly, S-CURVE eases in and out. The IN field next to STORE sets the fade of a new cue.
