# Undo and redo
keywords: undo, redo, history, mistake, revert, go back, ctrl+z, ctrl+y

Almost every change is one undo step: placing, moving or deleting items, patching, storing cues, palettes and groups, effects and programmer changes. Undo with Ctrl+Z and redo with Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z (Edit menu, or change the keys in Preferences › Keys). The assistant can do it too: "undo" or "redo".

Playing cues, moving faders and the masters are live playback, not edits, so they are not undone.
