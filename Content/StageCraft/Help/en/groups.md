# Groups
keywords: group, groups, saved selection, record group, select group, rename group
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Groups
control: RecordGroup

A group is a saved selection of fixtures. Select fixtures and press RECORD in the Groups panel to record a new group. Click a group's tile to select its fixtures again, in the order they were recorded (the order FAN and effects follow).

Pick a group to rename it (type the name and press Enter), UPDATE it with the current selection, or DELETE it. Deleting a group never removes its fixtures. Every change is undoable.
