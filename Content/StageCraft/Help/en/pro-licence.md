# StageCraft Pro: subscription and licence
keywords: pro, subscription, licence, license, subscribe, monthly, yearly, offline, renew, refresh, release, cancel, billing, invoice, free, universes, locked
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Preferences

StageCraft is free for designing: stages, rigging, lighting, cues, paperwork, GDTF and MVR import, and one universe of DMX OUT. StageCraft Pro adds unlimited DMX OUT universes, DMX IN (Art-Net and sACN input), MIDI and OSC control, the Web Remote and MVR export. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription.

- Subscribe in Preferences > STAGECRAFT PRO. Sign in to StageCraft Cloud first (Marketplace > SIGN IN). MONTHLY or YEARLY opens the checkout in your browser; StageCraft never sees your card. Pro switches on by itself when the payment is confirmed.
- Pro works offline for 14 days at a time. Each time StageCraft is open and signed in, it renews the licence on this computer (at start, at sign-in and every 12 hours). REFRESH renews it now, for example before a show without internet.
- When the licence runs out, the Pro features lock. Your scenes stay fully usable: fixtures, the patch and every universe are kept, and DMX OUT keeps sending the lowest universe. MIDI, OSC and the Web Remote close, so no port stays open for them. Renew the licence and everything works again, with your settings.
- Free DMX OUT sends the lowest patched universe. The Patch panel names the universes that are held back.
- One subscription covers up to 3 computers. RELEASE THIS PC frees this computer's place, for example before you replace it.
- MANAGE opens your subscription in the browser: cancel, change the card or download invoices. A cancelled subscription stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
- The licence belongs to this computer and this Windows user. Copied to another computer it does not work; sign in there instead.
- If StageCraft says the computer's clock is set before the last time it ran, correct the date, or refresh the licence online.
