# Flash limit, AUDIO master and KILL
keywords: flash, flash limit, strobe, seizure, epilepsy, photosensitive, safety, audio master, kill, latency, delay, offset
panel: StageCraft.Panel.Audio
control: FlashLimit

Effects that follow the music are limited so a light may flash from dark to bright at most 3 times a second, on screen and on DMX OUT, and a strobe may run no faster. Change it with FLASH in the Audio panel (1-10). Above 3 a warning shows: faster flashing can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, so keep 3 when an audience can see the lights.

The AUDIO master (Playback and Effects panels) sets how much those effects follow the music; KILL stops them following it at once (pulses stay open, chases hold). BLACKOUT is the way to dark.

LATENCY moves the lights against the sound for the current source, if they land before or after the beat you hear.
